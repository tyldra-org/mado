The quiet web: notes on browsing

Journal · 6 min read

A browser should get out of the way. The page is the thing you came for; everything around it is there to help you get to the next page, and then step back.

That means a chrome that is calm, controls that appear when you reach for them, and prompts that ask once, clearly, and remember the answer.

It also means the browser has to be honest about what it is doing: which site you are on, whether the connection is private, and what an agent did in your name.
